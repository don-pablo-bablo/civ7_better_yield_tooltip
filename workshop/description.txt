Shows where each of a tile's yields comes from: base tile, adjacency, buildings, Great Works, policies, pantheon, civ and leader abilities, specialists and appeal.

[h2]How to use it[/h2]
With a mouse:
[olist]
[*]Hover a tile.
[*]Press [b]K[/b] to lock the tooltip.
[*]Hover the yield icons at the bottom of the tooltip.
[/olist]

With a controller:
[olist]
[*]Move the cursor over a tile.
[*]Click the left stick to lock the tooltip.
[*]Press left or right on the d-pad until the breakdown opens.
[/olist]

A panel opens with the tile's sources, then each building's.

Some amounts reach the tooltip without a source. The mod names the bonus that matches the amount exactly. If more than one could match, the line is dimmed and lists them all. Amounts with no match are shown as [b]Unattributed[/b].

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
[list]
[*]The mod changes the interface only. You can add it to a game in progress or remove it.
[*]It replaces the tooltip's yield bar, so it clashes with any other mod that does the same.
[/list]
